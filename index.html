Platform advice

Which platform for a new business website?

A quick comparison to guide the decision. Our usual recommendation for service businesses: Webflow — then tailor the choice to your needs.

There is no single “best” platform — the right choice depends on your content, budget and growth plans. Below is how we weigh the four most common options for a brand-new service business.

Our recommendation: Webflow

Best balance of design freedom, content management and speed-to-launch for a new service business. WordPress suits content-heavy or budget-sensitive projects; Shopify wins for e-commerce from day one.

Platform Best for Design freedom Cost & upkeep Speed to launch
Webflow Design-led service sites High — pixel-level control Monthly plan · low upkeep Fast — no hosting to manage
WordPress Content-heavy & budget-conscious Medium — themes & plugins Cheapest long-term · more upkeep Medium — more configuration
Shopify E-commerce from day one Medium — strong store toolkit Transaction fees apply Fast — but locked to its ecosystem
Custom static Showcase & portfolio sites Total — built from scratch Lowest — static hosting Fast — if content stays simple

This comparison is an illustrative recommendation for portfolio demonstration — pricing and features change over time and should be verified before a decision.
